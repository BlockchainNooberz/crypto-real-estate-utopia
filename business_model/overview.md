# Business Model Overview

## Revenue Streams

### 1. Token Sale (Primary Raise)
- 10M CREU tokens at $0.05–$0.50 (graduated pricing)
- Target raise: $50M–$200M
- Use of proceeds: Land (40%), Construction (35%), Operations (15%), Reserve (10%)

### 2. Resort Operations Revenue
- Hotel room revenue (nightly rates $300–$2,000)
- Restaurant & F&B operations
- Spa, marina, and recreational facilities
- Private villa rentals

### 3. Yield Distribution
- 70% of net operating income → token holders (via XRP dividends)
- 20% → reserve fund
- 10% → development team

### 4. Secondary Market
- 2% transaction fee on all secondary CREU trades
- Projected volume: $10M–$50M annually at maturity
